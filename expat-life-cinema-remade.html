The rules this remake follows

Every place gets an image-first slide set: verdict + rating, at least four stay slides (with more when a source publishes distinct housing photos), up to two social slides, several meal slides, up to two gym/recovery slides, up to two activity slides, a readable map with five named real property/venue pins, itemized $2K/$3K budgets, and a sample day. Live price chips are shown in U.S. dollars. A category is called SOURCE + PRICE + IMAGE VERIFIED only when the source, dated price, and source-specific image were found together; vacancy and date-window language remains visible when it still needs confirmation. When that exact source does not publish a clean photo, the deck shows a real, location-matched REAL LOCATION REFERENCE and names the photo source; it never presents that reference as proof of the exact rental, plate, event, or membership. Map pins carry a direct venue/property source and mark exact versus approximate coordinates; rental platforms often mask the exact apartment address. The short-stay check window is Oct 13–Nov 12, 2026 for one guest; prices and availability are snapshots, not guarantees. Ratings are planning scores, not review-site scores: overall fit = 18% value + 18% social + 18% nature + 16% training + 12% food + 18% access. Use the linked source buttons before booking or paying.
