The rules this remake follows

Every place gets eight slides: verdict + rating, four stay visuals, social and activity visuals with a schedule lead, restaurant interior + dish visuals with a price lead, gym + recovery visuals with a membership lead, a readable geographic map with five named pins, itemized $2K/$3K budgets, and a sample day. Photos are marked visual reference unless a named venue source is attached; that keeps a pretty image from pretending to be a confirmed listing. Ratings are planning scores, not review-site scores: overall fit = 18% value + 18% social + 18% nature + 16% training + 12% food + 18% access. Use the linked source buttons before booking or paying.
