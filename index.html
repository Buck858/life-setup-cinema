The rules this remake follows

Every place gets fourteen no-scroll slides: verdict + rating, four stay slides, two social slides, two meal slides, two gym/recovery slides, a readable map with five named real property/venue pins, itemized $2K/$3K budgets, and a sample day. Live price chips are shown in U.S. dollars. A category is called LIVE SOURCE VERIFIED only when the source, dated price, and source-specific image were found together; partial or missing evidence is labeled on-slide and never replaced with stock photography. Map pins carry a direct venue/property source and mark exact versus approximate coordinates; rental platforms often mask the exact apartment address. The short-stay check window is Oct 13–Nov 12, 2026 for one guest; prices and availability are snapshots, not guarantees. Ratings are planning scores, not review-site scores: overall fit = 18% value + 18% social + 18% nature + 16% training + 12% food + 18% access. Use the linked source buttons before booking or paying.
